# Unit 8: Integral Calculus

Official 2026 text: "Integral as an anti-derivative, Fundamental integrals involving algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic functions. Integration by substitution, by parts and by partial fractions. Integration using trigonometric identities. Evaluation of simple integrals of the type dx/(x^2 +/- a^2), dx/sqrt(x^2 +/- a^2), dx/(a^2 - x^2), dx/sqrt(a^2 - x^2), dx/(ax^2 + bx + c), dx/sqrt(ax^2 + bx + c), (px + q)/(ax^2 + bx + c) dx, (px + q)/sqrt(ax^2 + bx + c) dx, sqrt(a^2 +/- x^2) dx, sqrt(x^2 - a^2) dx. The fundamental theorem of calculus, properties of definite integrals. Evaluation of definite integrals, determining areas of the regions bounded by simple curves in standard forms."

In scope (interpretation used for this bank):
- Indefinite integrals: fundamental integrals, substitution, trigonometric identities, by parts, e^x(f + f') form, partial fractions, the listed special forms
- Definite integrals: fundamental theorem, properties (King's property, even/odd, periodicity, splitting at points of non-smoothness), greatest integer and modulus integrands, derivative of an integral with variable limits
- Areas bounded by simple curves: lines, parabolas, circles, ellipses, modulus curves, between two curves

Out of scope (do not write):
- Reduction formulae
- definite integral as a limit of a sum (avoid)
- Walli's formula by name
- double integrals
