# Unit 9: Differential Equations

Official 2026 text: "Ordinary differential equations, their order and degree, the solution of differential equation by the method of separation of variables, solution of a homogeneous and linear differential equation."

In scope (interpretation used for this bank):
- Order and degree (including when degree is undefined)
- variables separable, including substitutions reducing to separable
- homogeneous equations
- linear first-order equations, integrating factor
- simple applications (growth/decay, curves with given slope property) solved by these methods

Out of scope (do not write):
- Formation of differential equations by eliminating constants (not listed)
- Bernoulli equations
- exact equations
- second-order equations
