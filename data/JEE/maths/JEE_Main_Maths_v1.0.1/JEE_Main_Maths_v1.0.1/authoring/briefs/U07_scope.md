# Unit 7: Limit, Continuity and Differentiability

Official 2026 text: "Real-valued functions, algebra of functions; polynomial, rational, trigonometric, logarithmic and exponential functions; inverse functions. Graphs of simple functions. Limits, continuity and differentiability. Differentiation of the sum, difference, product and quotient of two functions. Differentiation of trigonometric, inverse trigonometric, logarithmic, exponential, composite and implicit functions; derivatives of order upto two, Applications of derivatives: Rate of change of quantities, monotonic-Increasing and decreasing functions, Maxima and minima of functions of one variable."

In scope (interpretation used for this bank):
- Real functions: domain, range, algebra of functions, even/odd, periodicity of simple functions, inverse of a function, graphs of simple functions (modulus, greatest integer, signum, exponential, log)
- Limits: algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic standard limits, 1^infinity form, left and right limits, limits at infinity
- Continuity and differentiability at a point and on intervals, piecewise functions, greatest integer and modulus functions, parameter values for continuity/differentiability
- Differentiation rules, chain rule, implicit, inverse trigonometric, logarithmic differentiation, second derivatives
- Applications: rate of change, increasing/decreasing, local and absolute maxima/minima of one-variable functions

Out of scope (do not write):
- Parametric differentiation (not listed)
- L'Hospital's rule may be used in solutions but not named as required
- tangents and normals (not listed)
- Rolle's and mean value theorems (not listed)
- approximations by differentials
