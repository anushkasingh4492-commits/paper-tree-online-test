# Unit 13: Statistics and Probability

Official 2026 text: "Measures of dispersion; calculation of mean, median, mode of grouped and ungrouped data, calculation of standard deviation, variance and mean deviation for grouped and ungrouped data. Probability: Probability of an event, addition and multiplication theorems of probability, Baye's theorem, probability distribution of a random variable."

In scope (interpretation used for this bank):
- Mean, median, mode of grouped and ungrouped data, mean deviation about mean/median, variance and standard deviation, effect of change of origin and scale, combined mean and variance, corrected values after a wrong observation
- Probability of events, addition theorem, multiplication theorem, conditional probability, independent events
- Total probability and Bayes' theorem, probability distribution of a discrete random variable, its mean and variance

Out of scope (do not write):
- Binomial distribution by name (the 2026 syllabus does not list it); Bernoulli trials only as a plain probability calculation
- correlation and regression
- continuous distributions
- coefficient of variation is allowed as a ratio only
