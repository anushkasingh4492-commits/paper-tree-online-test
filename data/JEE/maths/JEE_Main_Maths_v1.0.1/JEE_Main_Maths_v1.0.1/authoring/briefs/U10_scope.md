# Unit 10: Co-ordinate Geometry

Official 2026 text: "Cartesian system of rectangular coordinates in a plane, distance formula, sections formula, locus and its equation, the slope of a line, parallel and perpendicular lines, intercepts of a line on the co-ordinate axis. Straight line: Various forms of equations of a line, intersection of lines, angles between two lines, conditions for concurrence of three lines, the distance of a point from a line, co-ordinate of the centroid, orthocentre and circumcentre of a triangle. Circle, conic sections: A standard form of equations of a circle, the general form of the equation of a circle, its radius and centre, equation of a circle when the endpoints of a diameter are given, points of intersection of a line and a circle with the centre at the origin and sections of conics, equations of conic sections (parabola, ellipse and hyperbola) in standard forms."

In scope (interpretation used for this bank):
- Distance, section formula, locus, slope, parallel/perpendicular, intercepts, centroid/orthocentre/circumcentre/incentre of a triangle, area of triangle by coordinates
- Lines: all standard forms, intersection, angle between lines, concurrency, distance of a point from a line, distance between parallel lines, foot of perpendicular and image of a point in a line, family of lines through an intersection
- Circles: standard and general forms, centre and radius, diameter form, circle through three points, intersection of a line with a circle centred at the origin (including the tangency condition c^2 = a^2(1 + m^2) derived from intersection), position of a point
- Parabola, ellipse, hyperbola in standard forms: focus, directrix, vertex, axes, eccentricity, latus rectum, focal distances, identifying a conic from its equation, finding equation from given data, intersection of a line with a conic as a quadratic

Out of scope (do not write):
- Tangents and normals to conics as a separate theory (not listed)
- chord of contact, pole/polar
- family of circles and radical axis
- parametric forms beyond simple use
- pair of straight lines
- rotation of axes
