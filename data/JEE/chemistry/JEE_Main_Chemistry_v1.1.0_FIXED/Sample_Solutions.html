1. Section A

Which description best identifies Wurtz reaction?

3. Section A

Which description best identifies Henry law?

A. gas solubility in a liquid is proportional to gas partial pressure at constant temperature

B. a solution with zero enthalpy and zero volume change on mixing

C. partial vapour pressure of a volatile component equals its mole fraction times pure-component vapour pressure

D. vapour pressure exceeds the ideal value

Answer: A

Henry law is gas solubility in a liquid is proportional to gas partial pressure at constant temperature. In the form p equals kh x, larger kh means lower solubility at fixed pressure.

25. Section B

A 2.52 g mixture of Mg and Al dissolves completely in excess dilute HCl, releasing 0.12 mol H₂. Assume no oxide coating or impurities. Use Mg=24 and Al=27 g mol⁻¹. What percentage of the original mass was Al? Enter the numerical value in the requested units, rounded to the nearest integer.

Answer: 43

Mg+2H⁺ gives one H₂ per Mg; 2Al+6H⁺ gives three H₂. Thus 24x+27y=2.52 and x+1.5y=0.12. Solve y=(24×0.12−2.52)/9=0.04 mol. Al percentage=27y/2.52×100=42.85714%.

Numerical entry: apply the stated factor 100, giving 42.85714; round to 43.
